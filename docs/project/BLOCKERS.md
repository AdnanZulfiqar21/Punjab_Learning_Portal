# BLOCKERS — genuine external inputs

A blocker here is a missing **external** input (person, account, approval, decision) that prevents a specific piece of mandatory work. It is never counted as passing evidence (roadmap §7.2). Everything not named here proceeds.

**Scope rule (owner, 2026-10-07):** missing reviewers or publication rights block only *academic publication and qualification* (approving or publishing lessons, questions, rubrics and videos, the RCS, Content Ready, marking qualification). They do **not** block any software: authentication, CMS authoring/review workflows, question/rubric schemas, test builders, durable submissions, scan handling, allowance accounting. Unapproved academic material stays private in draft/review states, and no reviewer identity, approval or qualification evidence is ever invented. Repository visibility (B12) and provisional branding (B11) are not development blockers.
