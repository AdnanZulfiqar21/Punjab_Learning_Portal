# Subject Index — Class 11 and Class 12

Hand-curated. Organises the dataset **by subject**, showing each class's book and chapters and how numbering carries across classes. Topic depth: `CHAPTER_TOPIC_INDEX.md`; deep chapter notes: `books/<BOOK_ID>.md`.

**Book ID convention:** `C<class>-<SUBJECT>` with subjects `PHY`, `CHEM`, `BIO`, `MATH`, `CS`. Chapter keys are `<BOOK_ID>/Ch<n>`, e.g. `C12-BIO/Ch18`.

## Subjects at a glance

| Subject | Class 11 book | Ch. | Class 12 book | Ch. | Class 12 numbering | Typical academic track |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Physics | C11-PHY | 1–12 | C12-PHY | 13–21 | **Continues** from Class 11 | Pre-Medical, Pre-Engineering, ICS (Phy) |
| Chemistry | C11-CHEM | 1–16 | C12-CHEM | 17–33 | **Continues** from Class 11 | Pre-Medical, Pre-Engineering |
| Biology | C11-BIO | 1–12 | C12-BIO | 13–25 | **Continues** from Class 11 | Pre-Medical |
| Mathematics | C11-MATH | 1–14 (Units) | C12-MATH | 1–11 (Units) | **Restarts** at 1 | Pre-Engineering, ICS |
| Computer Science | C11-CS | 1–9 (Units) | C12-CS ("Computer Science and Entrepreneurship") | 1–9 | **Restarts** at 1 | ICS |

> ⚠ Because Mathematics and Computer Science restart at 1 in Class 12, a bare "Chapter 3" is ambiguous. Always resolve chapters with the class (book ID), never by number alone. For Physics/Chemistry/Biology a chapter number is unique across both classes, but still store the book ID.

Track mapping is planning context taken from the project roadmap (§3). It is not printed in these books.

**Cross-class overlaps** (similar names, different content; never merge them):
- *Physical optics:* C11-PHY Ch 8 covers polarisation and gravitational waves. C12-PHY Ch 15 covers interference, diffraction gratings and Bragg's law.
- *Electrostatics:* C11-PHY Ch 9 covers Coulomb's law, Gauss's law and DC circuits. C12-PHY Ch 16 covers potential, capacitors and RC circuits.
- *Nuclear and particle physics:* C11-PHY Ch 12 is mainly particle physics. C12-PHY Ch 19 covers binding energy, fission, reactors, fusion, decay and tracers.
- *Thermal physics:* C11-PHY Ch 6 (heat and thermodynamics) leads into C12-PHY Ch 13 (kinetic theory of gases).
- *Waves:* C11-PHY Ch 7 (waves and vibrations) is related to C12-PHY Ch 14 (SHM).
