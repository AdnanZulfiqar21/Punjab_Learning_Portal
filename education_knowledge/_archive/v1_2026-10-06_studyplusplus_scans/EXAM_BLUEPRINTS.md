# Exam Blueprints — Pairing Schemes, Paper Patterns and Model Papers

Hand-curated from the back matter of the **Class 12** books. Each was read from the page images, not only the OCR. Full detail, including model-paper notes, is in §5 of each `books/<BOOK_ID>.md`.

> **Authority caveat.** These schemes are printed in PECTAA **Experimental Edition** textbooks (July/Aug 2026). They are the textbook's printed scheme, not a BISE notification. The project roadmap (§3) requires board-exam profiles to come from an approved official source with two-person review. Use these as a **draft blueprint / practice-test template** only, until they are confirmed against the official notification.
>
> **Class 11 books contain no pairing scheme or model paper.** No Class 11 blueprint exists in the dataset.

## Common Class 12 science pattern (PHY / CHEM / BIO)
Objective 17 × 1 = 17 · Short 3 groups (8/12, 8/12, 6/9) × 2 = 44 · Long 3/5 × 8 = 24 · **Total 85, 3 hours**. This pattern can be stored as one reusable blueprint template with a per-subject chapter-allocation table (above).
