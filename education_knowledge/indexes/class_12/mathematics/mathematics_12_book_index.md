### Chapter 1 — Graphical Representation of Functions
- Pages: printed 1–33 · PDF 3–35
- SLO codes: No SLO codes printed; unit opens with an INTRODUCTION paragraph (PDF 3) instead of a learning-outcome list.
- Learning outcomes (paraphrased from content, 9):
  - sketch quadratic graphs from factorised form; predict a quadratic from its graph
  - classify algebraic vs transcendental (fundamental / non-fundamental) functions
  - properties and graphs of exponential functions (domain, continuity, positivity, range, intercepts, monotonicity, injectivity, horizontal asymptote)
  - define hyperbolic and inverse hyperbolic functions; sketch sinh, cosh; prove identities
  - properties and graphs of logarithmic functions; vertical asymptote; monotonicity
  - modulus function graph; graph of inverse via reflection in y = x
  - shifting and scaling transformations of graphs
  - solve exponential/logarithmic equations and inequalities with domain checks
  - applications: growth/decay, sound intensity (decibels), discrete and continuous compound interest
- Main concept: The unit builds graphical literacy for functions: sketching quadratics from factors and reading a quadratic from its graph, then the graphs and analytic properties of exponential, hyperbolic, inverse-hyperbolic, logarithmic and modulus functions.
