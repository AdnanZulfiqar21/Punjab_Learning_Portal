### Chapter 13 — Thermoregulation and Osmoregulation
- Pages: printed 1–11 · PDF 1–11
- SLO codes: none printed. Learning outcomes: 10 bullets ('After studying this chapter, the students will be able to:'). Paraphrase:
  - Describe the three elements (receptors, control centre, effectors) of homeostatic mechanisms
  - Relate homeostasis with negative and positive feedback
  - Define thermoregulation and explain its need
