### Chapter 2 — Force and Motion
- Pages: printed 19–50 · PDF 22–53
- SLO codes / Learning outcomes: No SLO codes printed; 'Learning Objectives' box on opener (PDF 22), ~9 bullets with bracketed sub-notes. Paraphrase (10):
  - Differentiate scalar and vector quantities
  - Represent a vector in 2-D as two perpendicular components
  - Describe dot and cross product with properties
  - Derive the equations of motion (uniform acceleration, incl. graphically)
  - Solve problems using equations of motion (straight line, free fall without air resistance, vertical/horizontal components)
  - Evaluate and analyse projectile motion (height, range, time of flight, launch angle)
  - Predict qualitatively how air resistance affects projectile motion
  - Apply conservation of momentum to elastic and inelastic interactions in 1-D and 2-D (karate chops, car crashes, ball and bat, rocket thrust)
  - Predict motion for elastic collisions (relative speed of approach = relative speed of separation)
  - Justify why momentum of a closed system is conserved while KE may change
