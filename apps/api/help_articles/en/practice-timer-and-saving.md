Your answers are saved to your account as you go.

# Pending answers
If your connection drops, answers you chose are kept on this device and shown as pending. They are sent automatically when the connection returns or when you reload the page. Do not close the page while answers are pending.

# The timer
In a timed test, editing closes at the deadline. Answers that reach the service within 3 seconds after the deadline are still accepted; anything later can be rejected, so keep your connection steady near the end.
