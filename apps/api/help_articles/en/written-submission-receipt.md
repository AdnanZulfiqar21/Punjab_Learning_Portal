Uploaded pages are not marked until you submit. Submitting seals the pages and your page-to-answer choices, and gives you a receipt.

# Your receipt
The receipt shows its reference, how many parts you answered or marked as not answered, and when the service received your submission. You can submit only once; after that the script does not change.

# Time limits
- In a timed test, write until the writing time ends. You then have 10 minutes to upload and submit.
- In an untimed test you have 24 hours from the start to upload and submit.
- If the upload window closes before you submit, the test is not marked and the allowance it reserved is returned.

> If your connection drops while submitting, try again. Submitting again gives you the same receipt; it never creates a second submission.
