Write your answers on paper, then photograph or scan each page and upload it to the test.

# What you can upload
- JPEG or PNG photos, up to 15 MB each.
- PDF files, up to 40 MB and 10 pages each. Every PDF page counts as one page.
- At most 10 pages and 10 files per test, and 60 MB in total.

> The same file uploaded twice is used once; it does not count twice.

# Getting a readable page
- Use good light and avoid shadows across the page.
- Hold the camera straight above the page and fill the frame with the page.
- Check that small writing, units and labels are sharp before you upload.
- A photo whose long edge is under 800 pixels is accepted with a warning, because small writing may be hard to read.

# Matching pages to answers
After uploading, choose which pages answer each part of each question. If you didn't answer a part, choose "I didn't answer this". Every part needs a page or that choice before you can submit.
