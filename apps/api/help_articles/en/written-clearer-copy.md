If a teacher can't read an answer, they may ask you for a clearer photo of the same page. If an answer looks blank, they may ask you to send a clearer photo or confirm you didn't answer it.

# What to send
Send a clearer photo of the page you already wrote. Do not write a new answer. You have 7 days from the request; after that the question is left unmarked and its allowance returned.

# What the teacher decides
A teacher compares your copy with the original and records one of these:
- Same answer, easier to read: your copy is used to mark the answer.
- New or changed work: the original result stands. You can answer the question again as a new practice test, which uses allowance like any test.
- Can't tell: the original is too unclear to compare, so the original result stands.

> Sending the same photo twice, or retrying after a dropped connection, never creates a second copy.
