A subject teacher marks your submitted script against the approved marking rubric for each question. Marks appear on the test page once the teacher releases them, together with the teacher's reasons.

# How long it takes
Each submitted script is given a marking due time of 48 hours. Busy periods can take longer; your submission is kept safely in the queue meanwhile.

# Pending and unavailable questions
- Pending means a question still needs work, for example a teacher asked you for a clearer photo. There is no final total until every question is resolved.
- Couldn't be assessed means the question could not be marked. It is never counted as zero, and its allowance is returned to your plan.

> Marks on practice tests are teacher feedback for your learning. They are not official board results.
